SERVNEX - PRIVACY POLICY
================================================================================

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

Rooks & Brooks Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates
the ServNex mobile application and related services (the "Service"). This Privacy
Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you
use the Service.

This Policy applies to Administrators, Engineers, Customers, and other users.
If you use the Service on behalf of an organization, your organization may also
have its own policies regarding data you enter about their staff and customers.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SCOPE AND ROLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Platform Operator: Rooks & Brooks Technologies provides the multi-tenant
    ServNex platform, subscription billing, authentication, and core infrastructure.

1.2 Organization (Tenant) Administrator: Each subscribing organization controls
    much of the operational data within its tenant (customers, engineers, tickets,
    branding). Administrators are responsible for lawful collection and use of
    data they and their users submit.

1.3 End Users: Engineers and Customers interact with features enabled by their
    organization's plan (attendance, location, tickets, etc.).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We may collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Account and Registration Data
    - Name, email address, phone/mobile number
    - Password (stored via secure authentication; we do not store plain-text passwords)
    - Role (admin, engineer, customer, owner)
    - Organization/tenant identifier
    - GST number (if provided)
    - Terms acceptance timestamp and status

2.2 Subscription and Payment Data
    - Selected plan name, billing cycle (monthly, 6-month, yearly, trial)
    - Transaction identifiers, payment status, amount paid
    - Payment method/mode as returned by the payment gateway
    - Invoice and receipt metadata
    - We do NOT store full payment card numbers or CVV on our servers

2.3 Branding and Configuration
    - Custom app name, logo images, color preferences, font selection
    - Referral codes generated for customer onboarding

2.4 Operational and Service Data (entered by you or your organization)
    - Customer profiles, booking/service IDs, addresses
    - Device and service ticket details, photos, PDF uploads
    - Engineer assignments, status updates, attendance records
    - Barcode scan logs and product identifiers
    - Shift and attendance report data
    - AMC and service history as configured in your tenant

2.5 Location Data (when enabled by plan and permissions)
    - GPS/coordinates for engineer location tracking and geo-enabled features
    - Collected only when the app requests permission and the feature is active

2.6 Device and Technical Data
    - Device type, operating system, app version
    - Firebase instance identifiers, push notification tokens (FCM)
    - IP address and server logs via cloud infrastructure

2.7 Communications
    - Support emails and messages you send to us
    - In-app notifications content and delivery metadata


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We use collected information to:

3.1 Provide, maintain, and improve the Service;
3.2 Authenticate users and enforce role-based access;
3.3 Process subscriptions, verify payments, send invoices and receipts;
3.4 Apply plan limits (customers, engineers, storage, features);
3.5 Enable white-label branding per organization;
3.6 Send service-related notifications;
3.7 Respond to support requests and legal obligations;
3.8 Detect fraud, abuse, and security incidents.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. LEGAL BASES (WHERE APPLICABLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Depending on your jurisdiction, we process personal data based on contract
performance, legitimate interests, consent (e.g., location permissions), and
legal obligation.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

5.1 Service providers: Google Firebase, payment gateways (e.g., ICICI), email,
    mapping/location SDKs when geo features are used;
5.2 Authorized users within your organization tenant;
5.3 Law enforcement or regulators when required by law;
5.4 Successors in a merger or acquisition with appropriate safeguards.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data is stored on cloud infrastructure with tenant-separated paths. We use
authentication, access controls, and encrypted transport. You are responsible
for securing devices and managing user access in your organization.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DATA RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We retain data while your account is active and as needed for legal, billing,
and security purposes. You may request deletion subject to applicable constraints.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction where applicable law
allows. Contact support@rookstechnologies.com with subject "Privacy Request."
You may disable location or notification permissions in device settings.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Service is not directed to children under 18. Contact us if you believe a
child has provided personal data.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data may be processed on servers outside your country where our cloud providers
operate, with protections consistent with applicable law.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Payment hosts, Firebase, and map providers have their own privacy policies.
Review those policies when using embedded or linked services.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. ORGANIZATION ADMINISTRATORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Administrators are responsible for notices, consents, and lawful use of employee,
engineer, and customer data entered into their tenant, including location and
attendance features.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. LOCAL STORAGE AND PAYMENT SESSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The app uses local storage for session, branding preferences, and payment
recovery. Hosted payment pages may use cookies managed by the payment provider.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We may update this Policy. The Last Updated date will change. Material updates
may be communicated in the app or by email.
